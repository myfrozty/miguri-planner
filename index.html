Where your plan is kept

        

          In this browser, and nowhere else. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is shared
          between your devices. It survives closing the tab, restarting, and updates to
          this page — it does not survive clearing site data, private browsing, or
          moving to another phone or browser.
          


          So: press Export JSON whenever you have something you would not want to
          redo, and keep the file somewhere ordinary. Import JSON loads it back,
          here or on any other device — that is also how you carry a plan to your phone.
          Importing or resetting can be taken back with Undo in the top bar.
